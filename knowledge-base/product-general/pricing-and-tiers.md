# Brillio Pricing & Plan Tiers

_Last updated: 2024-11-05 · Applies to Brillio Suite (Core, Analytics, Connect, Shield)_

Brillio is sold as an annual, per-seat subscription across four plan tiers:
**Starter**, **Team**, **Pro**, and **Enterprise**. All prices are in USD and
billed annually per active seat.

## Tier Comparison

| Attribute                     | Starter        | Team           | Pro              | Enterprise            |
|-------------------------------|----------------|----------------|------------------|-----------------------|
| Price per seat / month*       | $19            | $39            | $79              | Custom (from $149)    |
| Minimum included seats        | 3              | 5              | 10               | 50                    |
| Billing                       | Annual         | Annual         | Annual           | Annual (multi-year)   |
| Support level                 | Community      | Standard       | Priority         | Premier (dedicated)   |
| SLA (uptime)                  | None           | 99.5%          | 99.9%            | 99.95%                |
| SSO (SAML 2.0 / SCIM)         | No             | No             | Yes (SAML 2.0)   | Yes (SAML 2.0 + SCIM) |

| Brillio Analytics module      | Add-on         | Add-on         | Included         | Included              |
| Brillio Connect integrations  | 2 connectors   | 10 connectors  | Unlimited        | Unlimited + private   |
| Brillio Shield (security)     | Basic          | Basic          | Standard         | Advanced              |
| Audit log retention           | 7 days         | 30 days        | 1 year           | 7 years               |
| Data residency options        | US only        | US / EU        | US / EU / APAC   | US / EU / APAC + custom |

\* Per-seat monthly price is billed annually (e.g., Pro = $79/seat/month = $948/seat/year).

## Pro vs Enterprise — Key Differences (Sales Quick Reference)

The two questions most often asked by prospects concern the jump from **Pro** to
**Enterprise**. The material differences are:

- **Price per seat:** Pro is a fixed **$79/seat/month**. Enterprise is custom,
  **starting at $149/seat/month**, with volume discounts negotiated by the
  account team.
- **Included seats:** Pro includes a **minimum of 10 seats**; Enterprise starts
  at a **minimum of 50 seats**.
- **Support level:** Pro receives **Priority Support** (business-hours + P1
  after-hours). Enterprise receives **Premier Support** with a **dedicated
  Technical Account Manager (TAM)** and 24×7 coverage.
- **SLA:** Pro carries a **99.9% uptime SLA**; Enterprise carries a **99.95%
  uptime SLA** with financial service credits.
- **SSO:** Both Pro and Enterprise support **SSO via SAML 2.0**. Enterprise
  additionally includes **SCIM automated user provisioning/deprovisioning**.
- **API rate limits:** Pro is capped at **1,200 requests/minute**; Enterprise is
  **6,000 requests/minute** (raisable on request).
- **Integrations:** Both include unlimited Brillio Connect connectors;
  Enterprise adds **private/custom connectors** and dedicated integration
  engineering.
- **Audit & compliance:** Enterprise extends audit log retention to **7 years**
  and adds custom data-residency and BAA/DPA options.

## Add-ons

- **Brillio Analytics module** — $25/seat/month (free/Included on Pro and Enterprise).
- **Additional API capacity** — sold in blocks of +1,000 req/min at $200/month.
- **Extended data residency (custom region)** — Enterprise only, quoted per deal.

## Notes

- All tiers include Brillio Core (the platform) and unlimited projects.
- Nonprofit and education discounts of 30% are available on Team and Pro.
